Good Questions

Before you decide

When is the right time to buy insurance?

The right time is while you have the opportunity to plan ahead — not when uncertainties arise. Acting at the right time may help you access more suitable protection options, subject to eligibility, underwriting, policy terms and conditions.

Will you help at claim time, or only while selling?

My commitment goes beyond the selling process: I assist with after-sales service and through the claims settlement process as well.

How do we decide which plan fits my family?

We start with your needs, responsibilities, liabilities, dreams and aspirations — your income to protect, your health to secure, your wealth to preserve — and build the protection strategy around them. The two surveys above are a good first step.

What details do you need from me?

Just the basics to begin: your contact details and a few details about your needs, goals and aspirations. Use the callback form at the top of this page, and I’ll take it from there.
