ABOUT ME

With a distinguished professional journey spanning over three decades, a dynamic professional I have demonstrated consistent excellence across industries.
I began my career at STAR PAPER MILLS LTD., SAHARANPUR (U.P.), an ISO-9001 Certified Company & one of the major flagship company of DUNCAN GOENKA GROUP where I dedicated 30 years as a SOFTWARE EXECUTIVE, playing a pivotal role in System Networking Management, Servers Management, Softwares & OS installations & maintenance, Software troubleshooting & database Management & thus driving technology-led operational efficiencies, system integration and digital innovation.
Building on this solid foundation, I have transitioned into the Insurance services sector, where I am now serving as a Certified Insurance Advisor, AXIS MAX LIFE INSURANCE & NIVA BUPA HEALTH INSURANCE located at Saharanpur.
In this role, I would bring my deep understanding of customer needs, analytical thinking & personalized service delivery to help individuals and families secure their health & navigating them to stronger financial futures.
I am committed to helping you navigate your Insurance options to ensure that you & your loved ones have comprehensive life coverage & to offer tailored life insurance solutions, ensuring comprehensive protection and long-term wealth creation for my clients
My ultimate goal is to assist you in making informed decisions that can enhance your financial value & life security.
Therefore I would be delighted to assist you with a focus on helping individuals and families secure their financial future with a variety of life insurance & health insurance products tailored to your needs.
