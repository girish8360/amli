RIDERS IN NIVA BUPA HEALTH

WHAT ARE RIDERS/AD-ONS:
Riders (sometimes called add-on covers or endorsements) are optional enhancements you can attach to your base health insurance policy by paying an additional premium. They expand your coverage beyond what the basic policy offers.
RIDERS are additional benefits that can be added to a base insurance policy to enhance coverage.
NIVA BUPA HEALTH INSURANCE offers a variety of riders that provide extra protection and financial support in case of specific events or conditions.
Riders in Health insurance are optional add-on benefits that can be attached to a base policy by paying an additional premium. They help customise the policy according to specific risks such as critical illness, disability, accidental death, or premium-payment difficulties.

Why Riders Are Important

Riders are important because they allow policyholders to tailor their insurance coverage to better suit their specific needs and circumstances. They provide additional financial protection against risks that may not be covered by the base policy, ensuring that the policyholder and their family are better prepared for unforeseen events. By adding riders, policyholders can enhance the value of their insurance plan and have peace of mind knowing that they have comprehensive coverage for a wider range of situations.
A basic Health insurance policy primarily covers death. However, many financial risks occur during your lifetime, such as:

  • Critical illnesses (e.g., cancer, heart attack, stroke etc)
  • Disability (temporary or permanent)
  • Accidental death
  • Inability to pay premiums due to job loss or disability
  • Hospitalization expenses
  • Income loss due to death or disability
  • Long-term care needs
  • Other specific risks based on your lifestyle or family history
    •

    Riders help bridge these protection gaps. They allow you to customize your insurance plan to better match your life stage, health profile, and financial goals. By adding riders, you can ensure that you have comprehensive coverage that addresses a wider range of risks, providing greater financial security for you and your loved ones.
    Why add riders? They let you tailor your cover to your needs — e.g., if you want maternity, cash benefit, or cover for specific chronic risks that the base plan doesn’t fully pay for.
    Note: Riders usually must be opted at purchase or renewal time, and not all can be added mid-term.

    Riders & Add-ons

    Niva Bupa (formerly Max Bupa) has introduced several popular riders and add-ons that customers can opt for to enhance their base health insurance coverage:

  • Personal Accident Cover – Provides a lump sum if the insured suffers permanent disability or death due to an accident.
  • Critical Illness Cover – Pays a lump sum on diagnosis of serious illnesses (like cancer, heart attack) covered under the rider.
  • Hospital Daily Cash – Pays a fixed amount for each day of hospitalization.
  • Safeguard and Safeguard+ – Covers small or non-payable items (like conveyance, oxygen, gloves) during treatment that aren’t normally reimbursed.
  • Disease Management / Acute Care Add-Ons – Provides extra benefits like tele-consultations, wellness programs, or removes waiting periods for certain diseases.
  • Zero Co-pay Add-On – Removes the co-payment requirement (so you don’t pay a share of the claim amount out-of-pocket) except for room category differences

    SAFEGUARD+

    Safeguard+ Very popular add-on, especially with ReAssure 2.0 and Health Companion plans.
    Key Benefits:
    1. Claim Safeguard+:Will cover non-payable items mentioned in ‘List I,II,III,IV. Get coverage for many “non-payable” items. Clause for Reasonable and Customary Charges will still apply.
    2. Booster / Booster+ / No Claim Bonus Safeguard+: Cumulative Bonus under Booster /Booster+ or No Claim Bonus of the Base Plan will not be impacted at Renewal if total admissible claim amount in the previous Policy Year is not more than Rs. 1,00,000/-.
      Without this rider you may lose part or whole of that bonus/accumulated benefit when you make a claim.
    3. Sum Insured Safeguard+:Preserves the value of Sum Insured. Safeguards it against inflation. We will increase the Base Sum Insured on cumulative basis at each renewal by the rate of inflation in the previous year. Inflation rate would be the average consumer price index (CPI) of the entire calendar year published by the Central Statistical Organization (CSO).
  • Broader coverage: Non-payable items often include things like special equipment, extra diet, certain consumables, etc. These costs can add up even if they are small per item, and without it you might have to bear them yourself. With Safeguard+, you reduce “surprise out-of-pocket” costs.
  • Protecting your bonus / booster: ReAssure plans have benefits like Booster+ / No-claim bonus, which reward years of low/no claims by increasing effective coverage or sum insured. If you make claims under a certain threshold, Safeguard+ ensures you don’t lose these gains. This helps maintain value of your plan.
  • Inflation safety: Healthcare inflation is a major risk. Without the sum insured increasing, your base cover might erode in real value over the years. Safeguard+ helps maintain the purchasing power of your insurance.
  • Peace of mind: Knowing many otherwise excluded items will be covered, and that your booster/no-claim benefits are safer, gives more confidence in using the policy when needed.

    Other Riders

    Hospital Cash Provides daily cash benefit for each day of hospitalization (above 24 hours). Useful for covering miscellaneous expenses like travel, food, attendant charges that are not covered by base policy.
    Personal Accident Cover Add-on Covers accidental death and permanent total disability. Lump sum payout to the nominee or the insured, depending on the incident.
    Critical Illness Rider Covers a list of major critical illnesses like cancer, heart attack, kidney failure, etc. Provides a lump sum payout upon diagnosis, which can be used for treatment or lifestyle adjustment.
    OPD & Wellness Add-on (select plans) Coverage for Outpatient expenses like doctor consultation fees, pharmacy bills, diagnostic tests, etc. Also includes wellness programs, health check-ups, and discounts.
    Room Upgrade Rider Allows choosing any room category without worrying about sub-limits, depending on the plan. Especially useful for high-end hospitalisations where you prefer a single room or suite.
